Welcome to bodhimcnally.com. This site replaces my earlier GitHub Pages site and brings several parts of my academic work into one place: my CV, teaching resources, research interests and updates on my academic work.

The earlier site began as a simple home for teaching material. As my work across medicine, quantitative research and university teaching has developed, it has made more sense to maintain one permanent site rather than leave these materials across separate pages and documents.

I will use this section for occasional updates on research, teaching and academic work. Selected public LinkedIn posts can also appear here alongside posts written specifically for the website.
